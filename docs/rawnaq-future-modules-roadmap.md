# Rawnaq & Rawnaq Pro — Future Modules & Innovation Roadmap 🚀

This document archives high-impact, unique module concepts designed for architectural studios, interior design practices, creative agencies, and turnkey contractors.

---

## 🎨 1. Spatial & Visual Storytelling Suite

### 1.1. 🎨 Interactive Material Palette & Moodboard Builder
* **Concept**: An interactive visual builder allowing clients to select, drag, and mix luxury materials (e.g. Italian Carrara Marble, Brushed Brass, Fluted Oak Veneer, Microcement, Textured Acoustic Fabric).
* **Key Features**:
  * Live material swatch preview with texture zoom.
  * Real-time Aesthetic Index & preliminary cost bracket indicator ($\$ - \$\$\$\$$).
  * 1-Click **"Export Spec Sheet PDF"** or **"Send Moodboard to Get Quote Wizard"**.
* **Target Audience**: Interior Designers, Architectural Material Consultants, Luxury Fit-out Studios.

### 1.2. 🌐 Lightweight 360° Spatial Panorama & GLTF 3D Viewer
* **Concept**: Ultra-lightweight WebGL/SVG spatial room viewer without heavy third-party embeds.
* **Key Features**:
  * 360° render sphere/equirectangular viewer + 3D GLTF model inspector.
  * Gyroscope mobile motion support & touch drag navigation.
  * Spatial Hotspots pinned in 3D space with popup specifications (e.g. "Kitchen Island", "Master Bed Walk-in Closet", "Acoustic Wall Paneling").
* **Target Audience**: 3D Visualization Studios, Real Estate Developers, Turnkey Architects.

## 💼 2. Conversion & Deal Closing Suite

### 2.1. 📈 Interactive ROI & Cost-Savings Payback Visualizer
* **Concept**: Dynamic client pitch deck calculator demonstrating return on investment, operational energy savings, or asset appreciation.
* **Key Features**:
  * Dual-slider comparison: Existing Operational Costs vs. New Architecture / Green Fitout.
  * Animated SVG Break-Even Curve and Payback Timeline (e.g., *"Payback achieved in 14 months with estimated 22% property valuation surge"*).
  * Direct export & inclusion into official PDF proposals.
* **Target Audience**: Commercial Architects, Corporate Workspace Fitouts, Solar & Sustainable Engineering, Software Studios.

### 2.2. 📋 Live Client Project Milestone Tracker (Client Portal Widget)
* **Concept**: A client-facing project timeline portal for active / won deals.
* **Key Features**:
  * Progress timeline phases: `1. Concept Design` ➔ `2. 3D Visualization` ➔ `3. Material Procurement` ➔ `4. Site Execution` ➔ `5. Handover`.
  * Weekly on-site photo gallery log with milestone completion timestamps.
  * Change-Order request modal and digital sign-off.
* **Target Audience**: Turnkey Contractors, High-end Residential Construction, Project Management Consultants.

### 2.3. ⚖️ Scope & Deliverable Tier Comparison Matrix
* **Concept**: Interactive feature-by-feature matrix comparing project packages beyond standard pricing tables.
* **Key Features**:
  * Multi-tier feature breakdown with interactive "Included / Excluded" highlights.
  * Delivery timeline comparison, revision count toggles, and CAD deliverable lists.
* **Target Audience**: Design Agencies, Web Studios, Freelance Collectives.

## 🗺️ 3. Trust, Authority & Portfolio Suite

### 3.1. 📍 Interactive Vector Map & Global Project Pinboard
* **Concept**: Lightweight vector SVG map (World, Middle East / GCC, USA, Europe, Bangladesh, or Custom City SVG) showcasing completed project locations.
* **Key Features**:
  * Pulsing luxury map pins with cluster grouping.
  * Hovering/clicking a pin reveals a Bento project preview card with budget, sector, year, and 1-click Case Study modal link.
  * Filter pins by sector (Residential, Commercial, Hospitality, Institutional).
* **Target Audience**: International Design Studios, Real Estate Conglomerates, Global Consulting Firms.

### 3.2. 👥 Interactive Team Skill Matrix & Direct vCard / Booking Drawer
* **Concept**: Bento team grid showcasing lead architects, structural engineers, and creative directors.
* **Key Features**:
  * Interactive skill radars and completed project tallies.
  * 1-Click vCard (.vcf) direct contact download.
  * "Book 15-Min Consultation" drawer linking to calendly or built-in appointment scheduler.
* **Target Audience**: Multi-disciplinary Architectural Practices, Creative Agencies.
